M-PYRE

by History Of Sky

Riffbox firmware for the M-VAVE FM-1

It's not a groovebox, it's a RIFFBOX. From idea to song: record drums, bass, rhythm and lead guitar on the FM-1's four tracks, real guitar and bass samples through amp models and the band's own cabinet IRs, a pedalboard on the screen, a fully editable kit based on realistic drum samples, and live controls for realistic guitar performances.

M-PYRE in two minutes: everything in it rendered by the firmware. Music by History Of Sky: The Prism · The Quintessence · The Truth (Parts I–III) · Spotify · Instagram

5 ampsclean, crunch, high, lead, bass
11 fxboost to fuzz, octave, mod, delay, reverb
realguitar, palm mutes, bass, drums
palmmutes, slides, accents per step
